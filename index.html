Nordic Warriors Individual Points League · Private · 20,000 Chip Entry

Nordic Warriors is an individual points-based league with no attendance requirement. Play any or all games — your top 12 Hold’em finishes and top 12 Omaha Hi/Lo finishes count toward the leaderboard each quarter. Show up when you can, compete at your own pace.

Orion’s Belt Team Poker League Team League · Two-Month Cycles · Three Divisions · Once a Week · 20,000 Chip Entry
